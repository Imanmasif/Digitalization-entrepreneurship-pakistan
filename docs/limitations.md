# Limitations

The published study identifies several limitations that should guide interpretation of the findings.

- Digital-access measures are self-reported and may not perfectly capture actual use or digital skills.
- The PSLM data are cross-sectional, so temporal direction and causal relationships cannot be established.
- Unobserved characteristics may affect both digital access and entrepreneurial engagement.
- The analysis focuses on non-agricultural economic activity.
- Access to a device or connection does not necessarily imply productive or sustained use.

The large sample and national coverage support analysis of broad patterns, but they do not remove these methodological limitations.
