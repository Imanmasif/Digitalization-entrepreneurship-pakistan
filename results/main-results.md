# Results

All values below are taken from the published paper. They are reported as associations from logistic regression models, not causal effects.

## Baseline regression

The paper reports the following key odds ratios in its logistic regression models:

| Digital access variable | Reported odds ratio | Interpretation in the paper |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mobile access | 1.968 | Approximately 96.8% higher odds of entrepreneurship in Model 1 |
| Computer access | 0.546 | Approximately 45.4% lower odds in Model 2 |
| Internet access | 1.127 | Positive association in Model 3 |

The fully specified models continue to show the same broad pattern: mobile access remains positive, computer access remains negative, and internet access remains positive.

## Subgroup results

The paper reports heterogeneous effects across rural/urban and gender groups.

| Predictor | Whole sample | Rural | Urban | Male | Female |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mobile | 1.927 | 2.065 | 1.431 | 1.936 | 1.575 |
| PC | 0.525 | 0.515 | 0.574 | 0.536 | 0.373 |
| Internet | 1.160 | 1.244 | 1.121 | 1.198 | 0.712 |

The reported mobile-access odds ratio is highest in the rural subgroup. Internet access is positive in the overall, rural, urban and male groups but is below 1 in the female subgroup.

## Interpretation

The paper interprets mobile connectivity as particularly important for entrepreneurial engagement in resource-constrained environments. The negative association for computer access is discussed in relation to the stronger connection of computers with formal employment, education and administrative activities. Internet access has a smaller positive association, with notable differences by gender and location.

## Statistical caution

The paper explicitly notes limitations from self-reported digital-access measures, cross-sectional data and potentially unobserved factors. The findings should therefore be read as statistical associations rather than evidence that access to a particular technology directly causes entrepreneurship.
