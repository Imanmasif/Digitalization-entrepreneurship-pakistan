# Results Interpretation

The published results show that the three digital-access measures do not have identical relationships with entrepreneurial engagement.

### Mobile access

Mobile access has the strongest positive association in the reported models. The baseline odds ratio is 1.968, and the subgroup analysis reports positive associations across the rural, urban, male and female samples.

### Computer access

Computer access is negatively associated with entrepreneurial engagement in the reported models. The paper discusses this pattern in relation to the use of computers for formal employment, education and administrative activities.

### Internet access

Internet access has a positive but smaller association overall. The subgroup analysis shows a stronger positive association in rural respondents and a positive association among men, while the reported female subgroup odds ratio is below 1.

### Policy interpretation

The paper argues for differentiated digital-inclusion policies rather than treating mobile phones, computers and internet connectivity as interchangeable forms of digitalisation. It particularly highlights mobile-first connectivity, affordable internet and interventions addressing gender and regional barriers.

These interpretations are based on the associations reported in the published cross-sectional study.
