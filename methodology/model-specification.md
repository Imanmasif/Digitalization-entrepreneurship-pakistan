# Model Specification

The published study models entrepreneurial engagement as a binary outcome using logistic regression.

The main specification includes mobile access, computer access and internet connectivity together with province, region, gender, age, education and household income controls.

The paper reports odds ratios rather than raw log-odds coefficients. The interpretation is conditional on the other variables included in each model.

## Model family

The paper presents five logistic regression specifications. The first three examine mobile, computer and internet access separately, while later specifications combine the digital-access variables.

## Heterogeneity analysis

Separate models are reported for rural and urban respondents and for male and female respondents. These subgroup analyses are used to examine whether the association between digital access and entrepreneurship differs across population groups.

## Limitations of the design

The data are cross-sectional and include self-reported measures of digital access. Consequently, the estimated relationships should not be interpreted as causal effects.
